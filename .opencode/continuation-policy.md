# Continuation Policy

Do not stop abruptly after routine progress, validation, self-corrections, a completed task, or a completed major milestone. Complete the current safe step end-to-end, including relevant verification and evidence.

After every completed task or major milestone, use a question box to report the concrete result and ask whether the user wants to continue to the next master-plan step or stop. This question box is mandatory before ending the response or session, even when the next step is known.

If the user chooses to continue, proceed directly with the next safe master-plan step and repeat this policy at its completion. End only when the user selects stop, requests a pause, a required approval/blocker needs their decision, or the master plan has reached its true permitted end.
