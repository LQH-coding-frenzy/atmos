## Findings And Resolution

- The deployed frontend initially rendered deterministic mock weather, showed unavailable sign-in, and
  exposed nonfunctional search, navigation, saved-location, AQI, and comparison controls.
- Browser inspection recorded a React hydration error caused by locale-dependent date/time formatting
  without the dashboard timezone. The public favicon also returned 404.
- The source correction fetches live dashboard data through the Worker, uses the selected location's
  timezone for server/client formatting, renders an explicit unavailable state on failure, and removes
  mock-only or inactive controls and dependencies.
- Protected production promotion run `35524535197` successfully deployed main SHA
  `62c6f9e8b7ae85cde8fd165097774c22efcc709e`; its exact-build and production-domain smoke checks passed.

## Scope Assessment

- The gateway, dependency health path, Open-Meteo integration, Vercel frontend, protected CI, signed
  backup image, encrypted R2 archive, and verified ephemeral restore are meaningful CV evidence.
- Continuous backup/restore operations are intentionally excluded. The one-time restore evidence is
  recorded in `docs/evidence/restore-001/2026-09-19.md`.
- Azure Container Apps environment and Log Analytics declarations, plus notification queues, are not
  needed for the active demo. They are retirement candidates pending explicit owner approval; this
  audit did not mutate provider resources.
