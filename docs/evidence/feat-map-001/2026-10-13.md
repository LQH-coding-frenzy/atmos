**Date:** 2026-10-13

**Production deployment:** Cloudflare Worker and Vercel frontend promoted through protected workflows

## Implementation

- The gateway validates RainViewer's live metadata, preserves the provider-returned opaque frame paths, filters to the recent two-hour window, limits metadata to 64 KB, applies a five-second upstream timeout, and caches normalized frames for five minutes. A cache-schema version key prevents an older empty-frame response from surviving the path-format fix.
- Weather Watch presents the RainViewer radar overlay with frame selection, previous/next, user-started 15-second playback, opacity, and attribution. The tile source is limited to zoom 7; city previews remain usable.
- OpenWeather Weather Maps 1.0 current layers for precipitation, clouds, pressure, wind, and temperature are selectable when the gateway has `OPENWEATHER_API_KEY`. Tile requests go through the Worker, which allowlists layers and coordinates, caches images, and keeps the key out of browser responses and logs. These OpenWeather layers are current snapshots, not animated history or forecasts.
- No database changes or paid provider plans were added. The production key value was never read or recorded.

## Provider constraints

RainViewer remains limited to past frames (up to two hours, 10-minute intervals), zoom 7, and its free-use terms (personal and educational use). OpenWeather free layers are current-only; paid history/forecast products are excluded. Its Worker tile-miss limiter is per isolate and best-effort, not a global monthly quota enforcer.
