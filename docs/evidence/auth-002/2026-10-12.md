## Behavior

- The sign-in panel starts Google OAuth through Supabase's configured `google` provider, redirecting to the same application origin.
- The signed-in account panel offers self-service deletion only after the user confirms the exact account email and types `DELETE`.
- `api-v1` validates the caller through the publishable-key/user-JWT client, requires `last_sign_in_at` within five minutes, and derives the delete target only from the verified user.
- The Admin Auth client reads `PROJECT_SECRET_KEY` only in the Edge Function runtime and calls `auth.admin.deleteUser`. The key is never sent to the browser or logged; existing FK cascades remove app profile, saved places, history, and alert rows.
- Missing/invalid confirmation and stale sessions receive generic errors; no account identifier is returned. After successful deletion, the browser signs out locally and clears that user's local saved-place preferences and offline snapshot.
